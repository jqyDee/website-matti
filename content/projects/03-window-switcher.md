> Sometimes the best tools are born out of sheer frustration. I built
> WindowSwitcher9000 because I was tired of how slow and restricted native
> macOS window switching felt. I didn't just want to switch applications; I
> wanted a text-based, lightning-fast way to find specific windows across all
> my spaces. Since I already use `yabai` (a tiling window manager), I decided
> to build the exact tool I wanted to exist. This was my first deep dive into
> Swift and macOS development. To move fast, I leaned heavily on AI assistance,
> treating it as a pair programmer to help me translate my core logic and UI
> ideas into working Swift code in just a couple of weeks.
