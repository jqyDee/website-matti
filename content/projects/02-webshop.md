> The Webshop began as a collaborative effort at the University of Innsbruck to
> master software architecture. We built a full-stack system using Spring Boot,
> React, and an H2 database, prioritizing modularity and containerized
> deployment. Though the migration to GitHub fractured our contribution
> history &mdash; misattributing our work &mdash; the code remains a robust,
> functional testament to our team's architectural focus and technical
> discipline.
