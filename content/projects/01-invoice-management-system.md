> This application is the result of multiple iterations trying
> to solve a real-world problem for a family member's therapy practice.
> Healthcare billing can be tedious, involving repetitive tasks and specific
> legal requirements. While earlier versions of this tool got the job done,
> this third iteration represents a massive leap forward. I rebuilt it from the
> ground up as a modern, containerized full-stack application. By migrating to
> a FastAPI and React stack, I was able to deliver a robust, responsive system
> that completely automates their workflow &mdash; from patient management to complex
> PDF generation &mdash; turning a daily headache into a streamlined process.
